## Thanks

Thanks to everyone who tested builds on their own servers, reported what broke, argued about default values, and pointed out where the documentation assumed knowledge a first-time server owner wouldn't have. That feedback is most of the difference between a plugin that works and a plugin someone can actually adopt.

No names are listed here that haven't been given permission to be listed. If you contributed and would like to be named, open an issue or get in touch and we'll add you.

## Third-party components

NekoCore builds on and integrates with several third-party projects, including Paper, SQLite JDBC, the MaxMind GeoIP2 reader, PlaceholderAPI, Citizens, and Multiverse-Core. These are the work of their own authors and communities.

For what each one is, how it's used, and where to get it, see [THIRD-PARTY.md](THIRD-PARTY.md) and the [dependencies documentation](docs/en/DEPENDENCIES.md).
